Beetroot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Beetroot does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server holding your data. Every habit you create stays on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing. Beetroot has no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party SDKs that profile you. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information.

What the app stores, and where

The habits you create — their names, descriptions, schedules, goals, reminder times, chosen icons, and your daily completion history — are written to your device's own private app storage. This data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not readable by us or by any other app;
  • is not backed up to any service we control (your device's own operating-system backup may include it, under the terms of Google's backup settings, which you control);
  • is permanently deleted when you uninstall Beetroot, or when you use Settings → Delete all data inside the app.

Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications. Used only to show the habit reminders you set up yourself. Reminders are scheduled locally by your device; nothing is sent through a push server, and no push tokens are created or transmitted. You can decline this permission and still use every other part of the app.

Network use

Beetroot works fully offline and requires no internet connection to function. All artwork and fonts are bundled inside the app rather than downloaded.

The one exception is app updates: Beetroot uses Expo Application Services to deliver over-the-air bug fixes. When the app launches it may contact u.expo.dev to ask whether a newer version is available. That request carries only technical information needed to answer it — such as the app version, platform, and runtime version. It carries no personal information and none of your habit data.

Children

Beetroot is suitable for general audiences. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or erase on your behalf. You have direct and complete control: all of your data lives on your device and can be deleted at any time from Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If Beetroot ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that change ships, and the update will be described here with a new revision date.
